## Additional diagnostic finding

The formal project gate is 100% statement coverage, and it passed. A separate
non-blocking branch-instrumented run calculated 99.90% with 15 partial branches,
but the instrumentation slowed the 10,000-record timing check past its 5-second
acceptance threshold. That diagnostic therefore ended with 1 failed and 2,174
passed; it is not reported as a passing gate. The same acceptance ran without
instrumentation at 0.823268 seconds and passed. TD-035 records the need to keep
timing acceptance and branch diagnostics methodologically separate.
